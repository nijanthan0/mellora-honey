The Mellora Standard

The Purity of Raw Honey

Unlike industrial honey that is heated, ultra-filtered, and stripped of vitality, our honey arrives at your table exactly as the bees prepared it.

🌡️

Never Heated Above 38°C

Industrial processing boils honey, destroying precious invertase enzymes and pinocembrin antioxidants. We cold-spin exclusively at hive temperature.

🌸

Intact Flower Pollen

Every spoonful contains hundreds of micro-pollen grains that provide rich trace vitamins, amino acids, and distinct regional aroma bouquets.

🐝

Bee-First Stewardship

We never feed our bees artificial corn syrup or sugar water. We harvest only genuine seasonal surplus, ensuring our colonies thrive year-round.

🔬

Nuclear Magnetic Certified

Each batch undergoes independent NMR spectroscopy to guarantee 100% botanical authenticity and 0% sugar-syrup adulteration.
